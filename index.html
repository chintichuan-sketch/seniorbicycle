About SilverSpoke

We started in 2012 when our founder, Eleanor, struggled to find a bike that felt safe after hip replacement. So she built one.

Today we're a team of engineers, cyclists, and geriatric physical therapists working together to build the safest bicycles for the 55+ community. Every frame is tested for stability, every component chosen for ease of use.

Our promise: no age limit on adventure.

Refund & return policy

Last updated: March 2025

We want you to be completely confident in your SilverSpoke bicycle. If for any reason you're not satisfied, we offer a straightforward return process.

30-Day Test Ride

You have 30 calendar days from the delivery date to return your bicycle for a refund (less original shipping cost). The bicycle must be in like-new condition: unmodified, no scratches, and with all original packaging and manuals.

Return shipping

Buyer pays return shipping. We recommend using a trackable insured service. Once we receive the bike and inspect it, we'll process your refund within 7 business days to the original payment method.

Damaged / defective items

If your bike arrives damaged or you discover a manufacturer defect, contact us within 7 days. We will provide a prepaid return label and ship you a replacement or issue a full refund, including original shipping.

Non-returnable items

Gift cards, final sale items, and bike parts (saddles, grips) that have been installed cannot be returned.
